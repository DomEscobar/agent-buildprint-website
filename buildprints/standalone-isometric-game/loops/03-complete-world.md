## How to implement this loop
Read the host `AGENTS.md`, `.buildprint/next-agent.md`, approved `PROJECT_CONTRACT.md`, packet `02-identity.md` and `loops/loop-flow.md`. Resolve packet paths relative to immutable snapshots after bootstrap; host data paths are relative to the game root. Load only relevant pinned framework skills under the available tool policy. Run only authorized work, with concise progress updates and durable upstream receipts.

1. Expand the semantic authority into all protected regions, routes and instances. Compose material boundaries once for appearance and navigation; inspect water continuity, banks, entrances, clearance and deliberate quiet/dense areas. Select modular masks or composed artwork according to the contract, not because a helper happens to create 47 tiles.
2. Author original maps and acquire/author approved host-owned assets. Do not copy the framework's demo/example sprites, characters, palettes, layouts, asset URLs or hosts. Use measured materials/contact families from calibration and preserve provenance/license obligations.
3. Keep floors, solids, openings and decoration distinct. Use sparse Scene v2 levels/links for raised traversal when required, grid-rectangle proxy unions for compatible irregular solids, and actual headroom/landing evidence. A large sprite is neither a large collider nor proof of a walkable deck.
4. Begin static checks before their work/evidence and protect all placed instances, not only listed showcase objects. Inspect ground-only and dressed whole views, worst seams/repeated motifs, boundaries and actual arrival positions across desktop and contracted mobile regions.
5. Preserve readability and accessible controls as content grows. Check camera/picking intent and reach after resize/zoom/floor changes; name geometry the upstream flat layout tool cannot certify and supply the appropriate real host proof when authorized.
6. Keep a derived implementation-to-requirement mapping in host technical notes, with acceptance status only in upstream receipts. Do not add a second completion ledger. Fix repeated art/geometry causes with a changed strategy rather than hide failures behind foliage, filters or zoom.

## Building objective
Build the complete static world and asset population promised by the approved contract, using the validated assembly style as a production foundation. The player must be able to recognize regions and landmarks, read paths and hazards, and reach all intended approaches without unsupported geometry or accidental collisions. Ground composition must remain coherent at playing zoom even without decorative props, while the dressed environment adds purposeful density and identity. Full means every agreed region, route, interior/exterior distinction and asset family, not an inflated sprite count or a beautifully polished starting corner. The content outside calibration remains first-class delivery work.

## DO NOT
Use placeholders, functionless buttons or mocked/sample data as final product or live proof. Do not copy demo/example assets/maps, edit framework internals, invent APIs or commands, spend without approval, weaken protected criteria, fake independent review, or reinterpret calibration as the full deliverable. No tests/benchmarks/browser QA/independent review during packet AUTHORING; this loop describes future authorized game work.

## Minimum proof before moving on
Complete scope-to-content mapping and actual loaded scene/manifest bindings; current all-instance topology/geometry evidence; complete ground-only/dressed views including required mobile regions and real traversal approaches. Counts and sidecars alone do not pass visual quality. All unfinished requested content stays open.
